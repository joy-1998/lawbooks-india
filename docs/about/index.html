Our Origin & Purpose

Bridging Century-Old Legal Tradition with Modern Digital Research

LexBooks India was established in the chamber corridors of Bhagwan Das Road, New Delhi, by a cohort of Supreme Court advocates. For decades, practicing advocates faced a stark dilemma: either carry multi-volume physical tomes into courtroom arguments, or navigate fragmented, unverified digital portals.

Our mission is simple yet transformative: to democratize legal scholarship across every district court, high court, and apex bench in India. We combine the meticulous editorial standards of premier legal treatises with instantaneous, search-optimized reading software.

With the historic advent of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), our mission has become even more vital. LexBooks is at the forefront of providing side-by-side transition commentaries that equip trial counsels with instant clarity.

Institutional Pillars

Our Guiding Principles

The core tenets that guide every volume we publish and every digital annotation we curate.

Excellence in Jurisprudence

Every commentary undergoes rigorous peer verification by constitutional scholars and appellate practitioners to ensure unyielding doctrinal accuracy.

Uncompromising Integrity

We cite only official law reports and certified gazette texts. Our cross-references are free from bias, marketing compromise, or secondary distortions.

Democratic Accessibility

Legal knowledge must never be restricted to elite chambers. We ensure junior advocates, state trial counsels, and law students have equitable digital access.
