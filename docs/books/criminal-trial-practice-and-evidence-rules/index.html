Overview & Jurisprudential Scope

Mastering criminal litigation requires courtroom presence, psychological insight, and forensic command. Monica Khosla shares courtroom-tested techniques for framing cross-examination questions, identifying contradictions and omissions, impeaching hostile witnesses, and presenting expert ballistics and DNA evidence. The work incorporates the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) provisions, detailing custody chains for digital forensics, hash verification protocols, and mobile device extractions. Essential reading for defense counsels, public prosecutors, and trial bench judges.
